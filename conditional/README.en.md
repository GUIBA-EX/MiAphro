They retain strict WC≤1%, GT≤3% and the close-relative gate, but treat loose-model hits as diagnostics rather than a hard veto. High loose-model counts must be considered alongside the apparent strict-model specificity. Identical alternatives were excluded; the suffix 2 is an alternative identifier.
